Or click a column: the nearer end of the stretch moves to it.

With A=0, C=1, G=2 and T=3 a word is a number written in base 4 — two bits a base. There are 4k possible words and 4k shelves, so every word has a shelf of its own, and no two words ever collide.

Every word of every subject is filed on the shelf its number names, with the subject it came from and the position it sits at. Classic BLAST does this the other way round — it builds the table from the query and streams the database past it — but a database index is real too: it is what makembindex builds for indexed megablast. The lookup is the same either way.
